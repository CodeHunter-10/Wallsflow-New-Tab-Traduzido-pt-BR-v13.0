Hey there 👋

Wallsflow New Tab has a new release.

The five biggest changes are below, and you can try each one right here on this page. Nothing you do here touches your settings.

Wallsflow New Tab speaks your language

Everything in the extension is now in English, German, French, Spanish and Romanian. It starts in the first of these in your browser's list of preferred languages, and you can pick another in Settings, under Language. Try it: pick a language below.

Good Morning Search the Web

The clock starts in the hours your language uses, 24 or 12, until you pick a format under Clock. A settings backup now carries your language and your tasks too.

Tasks for your focus sessions

Start a Pomodoro and your task list opens beside the timer, so what you set out to do stays in front of you and nothing else does. The task you are on is written under the timer, and ticking it there brings up the next one. When the session ends, the timer asks whether you finished it. Try it: tick a task or add one, then end the session.

Tasks
    24:59 Focus

    Outside a focus session the list stays out of sight. In the extension, clicking a task's text edits it, and the arrow that appears when you point at a task moves it to the top. You can turn the list off, or delete every task, in Widgets, under Pomodoro.

    Your wallpapers can change while you work

    Until now an uploaded wallpaper only changed when you opened a new tab. If you keep one tab open all day, it never changed at all. Pick a time and watch the list below.

    Change every

    Pick a time above to start.

    In the extension this control lives in Widgets, under Background. The wallpapers it moves between are the ones you marked as Random, or your Day and Night sets if you use those. A pinned wallpaper always wins, so nothing changes while one is pinned.

    Find a bookmark by typing

    The bookmarks panel was a list you had to scroll. It now opens with the cursor already in a search box, and filters as you type on either the name or the address. Try typing git, or mail.

    Bookmarks

    Escape clears what you typed, and pressing it again closes the panel.

    Wallpapers change without a flash of black

    Swapping a wallpaper used to fade the old one away before the new one was ready, so the empty page showed through the gap. The new wallpaper now loads behind the old one and only takes over once it is ready. Press both to compare.

    Everything else in this release:

    • Music stopped following you between tabs Pressing Stop could start the music again in another tab about ten seconds later, and stopping the last ambient sound could move it somewhere else instead of ending it. Taking over playback from a second tab could also erase what you had just chosen to play. The way tabs agree on who is playing was rebuilt, and 21 tests now cover it.
    • Uploading no longer stops the rotation Every upload used to pin itself, and a pinned wallpaper pauses the timer, so adding a picture quietly stopped your wallpapers from changing. An upload now shows straight away without being pinned, and the rotation carries on.
    • Day and Night now switches in a tab you leave open The time of day was only checked when a tab opened, so a tab left open past 7 PM kept its daytime wallpaper. It now changes over on its own.
    • A dropped file that is not a picture no longer empties the list Dragging in the wrong kind of file used to clear every wallpaper card until the next reload. It now just says the format is not supported.
    • The weather stopped refetching on every new tab It was ignoring its own cache, which quietly undid a fix from the previous release.
    • A focus session could count twice With two tabs open, one Pomodoro could add two sessions to the day's count. The other tab now follows along instead of counting again, including when you start a timer somewhere else.
    • The wallpaper list is quieter Labels that repeated what the buttons already showed are gone, and nothing is written over the artwork any more. The file size and the duration appear when you point at a card.
    • The city list in Weather works again Picking a city by hand showed the search box, but the matches under it could not be seen or clicked. They can now.
    • Bookmarks open where you tell them to Bookmarks have their own choice of opening in a new tab or the same one, separate from your shortcuts. Until you pick something there, they keep following the shortcut setting.
    • Smaller fixes A saved playlist could appear twice in your library. Turning the clock off no longer hides the weather with it. Text colour and shadow now reach the Pomodoro labels. Ambient presets can go above 100 percent like the sliders always could. And anything that says Saved now checks that it really was. The clock and the greeting now fade with the rest of the page while you drag a wallpaper onto it.
